Our Heritage

Born from the Bazaars of Lahore

RANG KARAVAN — meaning "Caravan of Colors" — was founded in 2009 when Zara Mirza walked through the Walled City of Lahore and saw master craftsmen whose art was quietly vanishing.

We partner directly with karigar (artisans) across Multan, Sindh, Balochistan, and the Punjab — preserving centuries-old embroidery traditions while creating bags worthy of the modern world.
